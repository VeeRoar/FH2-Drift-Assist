# FH2 Drift Assist & Drift Suspension Kits 0.5.1

**One download includes automatic countersteer, Formula Drift 60° suspension and SLR DRIFT 74° ANGLE KITS.** Download **FH2-Drift-Assist-0.5.1-win-x64.zip**, extract it, run **Install.exe**, then fit either suspension in the garage.

Inspired by **CarX Drift Racing Online 2**. In the creator's FH2 playtesting, the combined setup feels very close to CarX when catching a deep slide, crossing centre and unwinding as the rear comes back. Quick physical steering responds to the controller or keyboard, while the assist accounts for front-wheel travel direction and carries the last supported steering range over jumps and uneven ground.

- Two adjustable suspension profiles scale to the car's axle loads. Compatible add-on vehicles receive missing kits on database rebuild.
- Seven in-game settings cover assist, strength, input speed, release speed, deadzone, front slip window and chase camera. English and Traditional Chinese are included.
- The default preset enables assist and camera, with 150% strength, 100% input and release speeds, 4% deadzone and a 12° front slip window.
- Included in the ZIP: the Windows installer, installation guides in both languages, corresponding source and licenses. Required external tools and their official downloads are listed in the guides.
